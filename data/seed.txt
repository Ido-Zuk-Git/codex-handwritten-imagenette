Procedure: take the Imagenette2-160 download (classes in the fixed order tench, english_springer, cassette_player, chain_saw, church, french_horn, garbage_truck, gas_pump, golf_ball, parachute = wnids n01440764, n02102040, n02979186, n03000684, n03028079, n03394916, n03417042, n03425413, n03445777, n03888257). For each class independently, list the *.JPEG filenames of train/<wnid>/ (resp. val/<wnid>/), sort them lexicographically, shuffle the list with Python's random.Random(20261103).shuffle (a fresh Random(20261103) for every class and split), and keep the first 100 (train) resp. 200 (val). The 100 train files are the 'visible train' set (pilot: only the first 1 per class is placed in workspace/data/train, the full set stays in hidden/train_full until --full); the 200 val files are the hidden test set, stored AES-256-CBC (pbkdf2) encrypted in hidden/test.tar.enc as test/<class>/*.JPEG.
Train/val split rule: the agent-visible images are split into workspace/data/train/<class>/ and workspace/data/val/<class>/ (both dirs exist for every class). Full run: per class, of the 100 staged images in the manifest order (i.e. after the seeded shuffle above) the first 50 go to data/train and the last 50 to data/val (500 train / 500 val in total). Pilot: of the 10 pilot images (1 per class), 5 go to data/train and 5 go to data/val; the 5 val classes are random.Random(seed).sample(sorted(class names), 5). Membership is recorded in hidden/manifest.json (keys pilot_split, full_split).
